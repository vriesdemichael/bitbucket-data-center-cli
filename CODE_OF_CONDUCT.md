# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, unpleasant-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from
  them
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it

Technical disagreement is welcome and expected. Argue about the code, the
design, and the tradeoffs as much as you like; the line is arguing about the
person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code review — and when someone is representing the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through [GitHub's private
reporting](https://github.com/vriesdemichael/bitbucket-data-center-cli/security/advisories/new)
on the Security tab.

That form is labelled for security vulnerabilities, and it is deliberately
reused here: it is the project's private channel to the maintainers, it does not
require publishing a contact address, and a conduct report sent through it is
treated the same as any other. Please say in the report that it concerns
conduct rather than a vulnerability, so it is not triaged as a security issue.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy
and security of the reporter will be respected.

Be aware of a real limitation: this project has a single maintainer, so a report
goes to the person it might be about. If that is the situation you are in,
[GitHub's own reporting
process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is the appropriate route, and it is independent of this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding consequences:

**1. Correction** — Use of inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed
unprofessional. *Consequence:* A private, written warning, with clarity about
the nature of the violation and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may
be requested.

**2. Warning** — A violation through a single incident or series of actions.
*Consequence:* A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no
interaction with the people involved for a specified period. Violating these
terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

**3. Temporary ban** — A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behaviour. *Consequence:* A temporary ban from any sort
of interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

**4. Permanent ban** — Demonstrating a pattern of violation, sustained
harassment, or aggression toward or disparagement of individuals or groups.
*Consequence:* A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
project.
